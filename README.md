# FUTURE_DS_02
Power BI dashboard analyzing Facebook/Instagram ad campaigns — tracking impressions, clicks, CTR, purchases, and engagement to evaluate campaign performance.
Key Insights from the Dashboard

1. How well did the ad campaign perform?
- Campaigns generated 340K impressions, 40K clicks, 2,031 purchases, 0.05 engagement rate, and 0.12% CTR.
- Reach was strong, but engagement remained low, highlighting areas for improvement.

2. Which posts or ads had the highest engagement?
- Stories ads performed best with 12.8K clicks, followed by Image (10.5K) and Carousel (10.2K).
- Campaign_50_Summer and Campaign_27_Q3 had the highest engagement rates.

3. What was the CTR and ROI?
- CTR peaked at 0.1235% in August, showing late month strength.

4. What can we improve for the next campaign?
- Focus more on Stories format, which outperformed all other ad types.
- Rework creatives for Video and Image ads to increase engagement.
- Target campaigns in the Afternoon, when users were most active.
- Improve underperforming months (e.g., June) with better targeting.
